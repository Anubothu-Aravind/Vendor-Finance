# Product

## Register

product

## Users
Internal accountants, finance managers, and business administrators of Vastrams. They operate in a busy, transaction-heavy office environment and require precision, speed, and clear data density to perform rapid entries and audits without cognitive fatigue.

## Product Purpose
To streamline trade payables and debt financing by tracking purchase bills, managing financier loans, recording payment reconciliations with a First-In-First-Out (FIFO) allocation engine, tracking cheque lifecycles, and outputting clean ledger and outstanding reports.

## Brand Personality
* **Precise**: Trustworthy, structured, and bulletproof calculations.
* **Functional**: Clean, data-dense, and highly readable.
* **Restrained**: Professional colors, stable alignments, and no decorative fluff.

## Anti-references
* **Generic SaaS Templates**: Avoid over-spaced card layouts, tiny low-contrast gray text on off-white backgrounds, pastel gradient buttons, and generic visual metaphors.
* **Over-animated Interfaces**: No intrusive animations, floating elements, or transition delays that slow down user interaction.

## Design Principles
* **Information Density Over Whitespace**: Prioritize tabular data, clear columns, and readable typography, drawing inspiration from high-density financial systems like Tally or Zoho Books.
* **Actionable Badging**: Use clear, high-contrast status colors (e.g., cleared/bounced/pending cheques, ongoing/paid/overdue bills) to immediately guide the user's attention.
* **Frictionless Data Entry**: Focus on key-navigable forms, conditional field inputs, and immediate calculations to support fast data-entry workflows.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
* **WCAG 2.1 AA Compliance**: Maintain color contrast ratios of at least 4.5:1 for body and placeholder text.
* **Keyboard-First Navigation**: Ensure forms, dropdowns, and pagination controls are fully navigable via standard keyboard focus and shortcut interactions.
* **Reduced Motion Support**: Provide immediate transitions or basic opacity fades for users who prefer reduced motion.
